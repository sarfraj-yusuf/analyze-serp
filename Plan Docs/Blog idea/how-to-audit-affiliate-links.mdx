# How to Audit Affiliate Links & Avoid Google Link Spam Penalties

If you run an affiliate blog or product review website, un-labeled monetization links can trigger Google Link Spam penalties.

Google's Webmaster Guidelines explicitly require all paid or commercial links (including affiliate links) to use `rel="sponsored"` or `rel="nofollow"`.

---

## ⚖️ rel="sponsored" vs rel="nofollow": What's the Difference?

* `rel="sponsored"`: Created specifically for affiliate links, paid partnerships, and sponsored content.
* `rel="nofollow"`: Used for untrusted external links or user-generated comment links.
* `rel="ugc"`: Reserved for forum or blog comment links.

## 📋 Checklist for Safe Affiliate Monetization

1. Always add `rel="sponsored"` or `rel="nofollow"` to affiliate destination links.
2. Maintain natural anchor text variations instead of keyword-stuffed commercial anchors.
3. Keep outbound affiliate links under 15% of your total page links.
